Why it's different

🕹️

Works with ARAM Mayhem

Reads match data straight from your League client instead of Riot's public API — which Riot deliberately blocks for ARAM Mayhem. Works for every mode.

📊

Actually finds the story

Pentakills, ints, AFK teammates, stomps, nail-biters, unsung support healing — rule-based detectors surface what's actually worth talking about.

🔊

Text and voice

Posts a Discord embed and joins your voice channel to read the recap out loud, right after the game ends.

🔒

Runs on your machine

No account, no server, no third party seeing your data. Your Discord bot token and API keys stay encrypted on your own PC.

Setup

  1. Create a Discord bot — go to the Discord Developer Portal, create an application, add a bot, and copy its token.
  2. Invite it to your server — under OAuth2 → URL Generator, check bot with Send Messages and Connect/Speak permissions, then open the generated link.
  3. Install and open Hex ARAM — paste your bot token into the Discord Setup tab and hit Test Connection.
  4. Pick your channels — choose the text channel for recaps and (optionally) a voice channel, or leave it on auto-detect.
  5. Map your friends — add each friend's League PUUID and Discord name so recaps call people out by name.
  6. Play ARAM — leave the app running in the background. When your game ends, the recap posts automatically.
